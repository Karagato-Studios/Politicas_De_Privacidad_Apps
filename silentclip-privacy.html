Privacy Policy for SilentClip

Application: SilentClip - Video Mute & Audio Studio
Platform: Windows 11 / Windows 10
Developer: Karagato Studios
Contact: karagatostudios@gmail.com
Last updated: November 1, 2026

At Karagato Studios, we take your privacy seriously. SilentClip was designed under the fundamental principle of Privacy-First and 100% Local-First Processing:

1. 100% Local Processing (Offline Operation)

SilentClip operates entirely on your local Windows hardware. All audio stripping, volume modifications, audio replacements, and audio extraction processes run locally via embedded FFmpeg binaries. Your videos, audio tracks, and personal media files are never transmitted over the Internet, uploaded to cloud servers, or shared with third parties.

2. Zero Data Collection & Zero Telemetry

SilentClip does NOT collect, track, log, store, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, IP addresses, usage statistics, or telemetry. We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, trackers, or cookies.

3. System Permissions & File Access

  • File System Access: SilentClip only accesses the specific video and audio files that you intentionally select via the file picker or drag-and-drop. Output files are saved solely in the destination folder chosen by you.
  • No Internet Requirement: SilentClip operates completely offline. An active Internet connection is never required to use any feature of the application.

4. Children's Privacy

SilentClip does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 years of age.
